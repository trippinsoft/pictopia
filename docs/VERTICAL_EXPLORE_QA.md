# Vertical Explore iPhone acceptance

Status: implementation prepared; physical-iPhone acceptance and native build compilation remain unverified. Automated logic/host-adapter results are separate from device acceptance. This is a free internal prototype, not a release-wide redesign.

## Install through Draftbit

1. Save your work in Draftbit.
2. Open **Publishing → Publish Destinations → App Store Connect**. This app’s iOS destination is configured with bundle ID `com.trippinsoft.pictopia`; keep that identity. No RevenueCat keys or Pro purchase are required for this experiment.
3. Open the top-right **Publish** dropdown, choose **Apple App Store**, use **Staging**, accept Draftbit’s next version/build number, then **Start Publish**. A new native build is needed to include this route. Existing Expo/native plugins suffice; no terminal or manual native setup is required. Follow any signing or upload errors shown in Publishing before continuing.
4. After successful upload and Apple processing, open Pictopia in App Store Connect’s **TestFlight** tab and assign the build to yourself as an internal tester. Complete any Apple-requested testing/compliance fields. This does not submit the app for public review.
5. Install Apple’s **TestFlight** app on your iPhone, accept your internal-testing invitation, and install or update Pictopia. Confirm you are installing the newly built version. Keep an existing installation when testing upgrade persistence; uninstalling removes its local records.
6. Launch Pictopia, finish the existing onboarding if needed, authorize photo access, then open **Home → Try Vertical Explore — 200 Photos → Start Vertical Explore → Let’s explore**. Limited access works with the photos you authorize; fewer than 200 is explicitly allowed.

Agent tools cannot start a Publishing build or install on your phone in this session. App Store Connect agent read access is off; build processing and tester assignment must be checked in Publishing/Apple’s interface. Do not record native results as passed until the actual build runs on the actual phone.

## Required physical-device pass

- With full access and at least 200 eligible stills, confirm exactly 200 fixed IDs; with limited access, confirm only authorized eligible photos, and honest smaller counts. Videos are excluded. Live Photos use their still image. Photos in the production queue/journals are excluded when the snapshot starts.
- Slowly pull upward halfway and hold: see the current/next photos move together in adjacent page slots. Move the finger back without releasing; the feed must follow directly. Release below the navigation threshold and confirm native snapping returns to the original. Repeat downward with the previous page. Complete both directions with long drags and fast flicks. Up advances without a keep decision; down revisits without changing marks.
- Swipe up through 20 photos, then down through 5. Discard left then immediately swipe up; repeat with right. Alternate up/left/up/right/up/left for at least 30 photos. Repeat at speed. Compare displayed photos with their stable IDs/sequence and verify each discard applies to the departing ID. Confirm no overlap, flashing, skipped IDs or doubled actions.
- After each sideways discard, immediately repeat halfway peeking in both directions, holding and returning the finger without release. Both retained neighbors must appear in the proper page slots; discarded photos remain in the fixed sequence until an explicit replay or restoration changes their marks.
- Cancel partial horizontal and vertical drags and try diagonals in both directions. Horizontal intent must block native paging; vertical intent must fail the sideways pan and leave scrolling to the native list. Once one recognizer wins, the other must not switch on mid-gesture. Canceled gestures make no decision.
- Flick up rapidly 10+ times, including catching a moving page with your finger. Every flick must land; the feed must never jump back to an earlier photo or ignore a swipe while saving. Hold a slow half-page peek for several seconds: it must stay put until you release.
- Confirm the previous and next photos occupy adjacent pages and loading does not block Next. Try an iCloud-only photo on poor/no connectivity. After eight seconds the unavailable message must let you continue. An unshown photo must be counted separately from explored photos. Previous/Undo lets you retry it later.
- Undo each direction; verify the correct photo and its prior mark return. Previous only changes position. Revisit a discarded photo with up and confirm its discard designation remains until restored.
- Open the small discard-count control. Review the grid, open a photo full-screen, restore it, and Restore All. No permanent-delete control is present. Restoring a photo invalidates its old undo entries; Restore All clears history but preserves position and encountered state.
- Background during a gesture, an animation, and a save. Resume; then terminate/relaunch after an accepted action. Confirm snapshot, position, marks, instructions dismissal and undo persist. An action killed before its transaction commits may replay; a committed action must never process twice.
- Change limited-photo selection in iOS Settings/Photos, remove a selected original outside Pictopia, and relaunch. Confirm inaccessible IDs can be advanced past and do not silently get replaced. The experiment must never trap you on one asset.
- Finish the snapshot and compare the persisted counts with the collection. Explored = left untouched + discarded; unavailable/advanced-before-loading is reported separately. Undo/Previous remain available from completion.
- Use Restart Test from completion or the discard collection. Confirm it explains clearing local marks/history/progress, clears them, retains exactly the same photo IDs/order and keeps the first-use explanation dismissed. Verify every original still exists in Apple Photos.
- Repeat in light/dark appearance, portrait/landscape, larger text, VoiceOver, and iOS Reduce Motion. Tap Next/Discard/Undo/Previous as the accessible alternative.
- Confirm the ordinary Swipe flow, existing timed-session resume, production queue counts and restore behavior are unchanged. Do not request physical deletion while evaluating this experiment.

## Limits to evaluate

No native binary compilation, physical-device frame timing, iCloud transfer, OS gesture arbitration, background termination, limited-permission change, VoiceOver or Apple processing result has been verified here. Adjacent preloading uses FlatList virtualization (five-page window, three initial/batch cells), rather than mounting/downloading all 200 originals. Each page uses the measured photo viewport height; native scrolling owns all Y movement. Horizontal activation uses ±12-point X activation and ±12-point Y failure before activation, then 64-point distance or 16-point movement plus 650 points/second velocity, with a 190 ms X-only exit. Vertical paging is never locked by saving: settled pages are persisted in the background in order, and a sideways discard only starts when the pager is at rest on that photo. Departed cards reset after the next page has scrolled in; unavailable/iCloud media has an honest loading/skip path. Undo retains at most 200 accepted exploration actions.

## Synchronization verification scope

Automated controller/repository tests exercise the requested action sequences, reversed animation/save completion order, duplicate momentum/release events, cancellation, fractional-offset peeking without a decision, Undo, restore, relaunch and no native deletion requests. These are host simulations; they do not measure UIKit/Android gesture arbitration, real finger tracking, frame timing or iCloud loading. The former renderer placed two photos in the same absolute rectangle and promoted a card based on per-card asynchronous callbacks; it could accept canceled animation completion and had no native page alignment acknowledgement. That layout is removed. Stable cells now isolate horizontal transforms and keep a separate fixed native Y slot per asset. Physical reproduction and verification of the reported overlap remain part of the checklist above.
