# Store submission drafts

## Listing

Name: Pictopia

Subtitle: Keep memories. Clear the clutter.

Short description: A calmer camera roll, one swipe at a time.

Description:

Keep the memories. Lose the clutter. Pictopia helps you review your photos and videos without rushing your decisions. Swipe right to keep, left to set aside, and undo whenever you change your mind. Browse by month or start a manageable 5-minute clean.

Your originals stay untouched while you review. When you're ready, check the deletion queue and authorize the request through your device. Deleted media may remain in Recently Deleted or Trash.

Manual cleanup is free, with unlimited review, no daily swipe limits, and no account required. Pictopia Pro adds local exact-duplicate analysis, similar-shot comparisons, best-shot suggestions, likely-blur review, and large-video prioritization. Analysis runs on your device; personal media is not uploaded for analysis.

Pro plans are monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Store prices and terms appear before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled according to your store's terms. Manage or restore purchases from Settings.

## Privacy / data-safety working draft

Photos/videos are accessed for user-authorized local review; not uploaded or sold. Local metadata, decisions, preferences, and derived analysis remain in the device database. No app account, advertising identifier, advertising SDK, or app-added behavioral analytics.

RevenueCat processes an anonymous purchaser identifier, receipts/transactions, entitlement and necessary SDK purchase information. Apple/Google process store billing. Confirm the precise final SDK data categories and diagnostics with RevenueCat's current privacy disclosures. Do not submit an unqualified “no data collected” declaration when purchase data is processed. Platform automatic device backup and Publishing-added integrations must also be reviewed.

Permission purpose: read only the photos/videos the user authorizes to help review their library; deletion only for explicitly confirmed selections with operating-system authorization. No camera or audio recording permission is required.

## Review notes draft

Core cleanup is available without subscribing. Use a disposable native test library. Set-aside decisions do not delete media. Open the queue and explicitly authorize native deletion. Pro is tied to one RevenueCat `pro` entitlement. Lifetime is non-consumable. The browser preview has file selection and review only; native features require the submitted binary.
